**Coverage on test set outliers under Isolation Forest (target $1 - \alpha = 0.90$):** Outlier coverage results using QNN as the shared backbone across all methods. Outliers are defined via Isolation Forest with $5\%$ contamination; inliers are the $20\%$ most inlying points according to the same criterion. Parentheses show $2\times$ the standard deviation; bold marks methods whose coverage is closest to $0.90$ within a $95\%$ confidence interval. Both CREDO variants achieve coverage closest to the nominal level in at least 6 out of 8 datasets, while competing methods show degraded performance relative to the LOF-based evaluation — confirming that CREDO's adaptability to outlier regions is robust to the choice of outlier definition.
