**Fixed $\gamma$ ablation study:** Sensitivity analysis over $\gamma \in \{0.01, 0.025, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.35, 0.5, 0.7\}$ evaluating SMIS, outlier coverage, and ILR across 5 datasets. The results confirm a clear trade-off: smaller values favor epistemic uncertainty capture and coverage in scarce regions at the cost of interval efficiency, while larger values improve SMIS but risk under-representing uncertainty where data support is limited. The default $\gamma = 0.1$ sits at a stable operating point across both objectives, and can serve as a principled starting point for practitioners wishing to adjust this balance.
