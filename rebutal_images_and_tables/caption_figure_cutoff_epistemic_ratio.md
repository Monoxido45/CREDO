**Conformal cutoff-to-epistemic uncertainty ratio:** Distribution of the ratio between the global conformal correction and local epistemic uncertainty across the first 8 datasets, for both CREDO variants. When below one, epistemic uncertainty is the dominant driver of interval width. This holds for the majority of observations across most datasets, with exceptions in *cycle*, *airfoil*, and *electric* — where lower epistemic uncertainty levels or greater need for conformal regularization naturally increase the correction's relative contribution. The fixed variant remains entirely below one across all datasets, reflecting a more conservative balance between the two components compared to the adaptive variant.
