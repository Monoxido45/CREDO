**Outlier-to-inlier interval length ratio (higher is better):** ILR results using QNN as the shared backbone across all methods. Here, outliers are defined using the LOF criterion with $5\%$ of contamination and considered inliers are the $20\%$ most inliers according to the outlier detection method. Parentheses show $2\times$ the standard deviation; bold marks the best method within a $95\%$ confidence interval (ties allowed). Preliminary results over the first 8 datasets show that CREDO consistently achieves higher ILR across all datasets, with the largest gains on smaller datasets such as *concrete*, *winewhite*, and *winered* — where epistemic uncertainty is most pronounced — confirming that the method maintains its epistemic adaptivity regardless of the architectural base model change.
